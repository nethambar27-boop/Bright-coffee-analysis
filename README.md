
# Bright Coffee Shop - Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data from Bright Coffee Shop to identify top-selling products, peak sales hours, and monthly revenue trends.
The goal is to turn raw sales data into actionable insights that support better business and marketing decisions.

## Key Findings

### 1. Top Performing Products
- Coffee is the highest revenue-generating category
- Tea is the second best performer
- Baked Goods and Drinking Chocolate show moderate sales
- Loose Tea and Packaged Chocolate are the lowest performers

### 2. Monthly Revenue Growth
- Revenue shows consistent growth from January to June
- June recorded the highest monthly revenue
  

### 3. Time of Day Analysis
- Sales peak during 07:00-10:00 morning rush and 12:00-15:00 lunch period
- The 09:00-12:00 3-hour block generates the highest revenue
- Late evening hours have the lowest sales activity

### 4. Revenue by 3-Hour Intervals
- Mid-morning to early afternoon is the busiest period
- Staffing and promotions aligned with these intervals will have the most impact

## Recommendations


### Marketing & Sales
-Promote Top Products: Run loyalty programs and social media campaigns focused on Coffee and Tea
- Bundle Strategy: Create "Morning Combo" and "Lunch Combo" deals to boost Baked Goods and Drinking Chocolate
- Revive Low Performers: Pair Loose Tea and Packaged Chocolate with best-sellers at a discount

### Business Growth
- Track & Optimize: Monitor monthly revenue and hourly sales to adjust promotions and staffing in real-time
- Extend Peak Hours: Test small promotions in late afternoon to shift some demand from peak times
